Hello, I'm Gabriel

I build products where the AI agent is the product — not a feature bolted on at the end.

I'm a Product Manager based in Essex, UK, with 3+ years across AI products, SaaS and growth. Since April 2023 I've been at Kirro, an AI-powered job search platform, where I own the roadmap and use Mixpanel analytics to decide what we build next.

Outside Kirro I'm the founder of Wandr, an AI travel decision engine, and the voluntary Growth Manager for Easy Producting, a community where product people grow together. My path into product ran through marketing and customer insight, which is why I still start every problem with the customer's evidence rather than the org chart.

I leverage AI tools like Claude and Codex to optimise my workflow — and I use Claude Code to ship fixes and improvements to the product myself, without waiting on a sprint slot.

04 — Work principles

How I work

01

Agents first

If the AI can't do a meaningful piece of the user's job on its own, it isn't the product yet. I design around the agent's first useful act, not around a form.

Replace: workflow / agent-first snapshot
02
◐ ▲ ⌘

AI in the loop, every day

I leverage AI tools like Claude and Codex to optimise my workflow — research, PRDs, analysis, prototypes — and Wispr Flow to think out loud faster than I can type.

03

I ship, not just spec

I use Claude Code to ship fixes and improvements to the product that improve my day-to-day — work that increased customer retention by 40%.

04

Evidence & honesty

Evidence over opinion: every roadmap call is argued from Mixpanel funnels and user conversations.

Honest about technology: I say plainly what a model can and can't do today.

05

Value-driven design

Screens serve the outcome. In a job platform like Kirro, the win is a completed application, not a beautiful listing page.

06

Ship, measure, iterate

Small releases, fast feedback. Wandr's PRD has been through four versions because the customer keeps teaching us something.
